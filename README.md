## 🌟 Overview / Panoramica

**RomaLab Scientifico** is the definitive scientific calculator and interactive cooking companion for authentic Roman culinary traditions:
* 🍳 **Spaghetti alla Carbonara**
* 🧀 **Tonnarelli Cacio e Pepe**
* 🍅 **Bucatini all'Amatriciana**
* 🥓 **Mezze Maniche alla Gricia**

Built with the same battle-tested, high-performance architecture as **PizzaLab Scientifico**, RomaLab takes the guesswork out of delicate kitchen physics:
- **Thermal Emulsion Window**: Real-time monitoring of the 58°C - 65°C safe zone (preventing the dreaded "scrambled egg / frittata" effect or rubbery pecorino clumps).
- **Mathematical Portions**: Automatically scales ingredients from 1 to 24 servings down to the gram or tenth of an ounce.
- **The Starch Formula**: Calculates water volume and boiling salinity reduction (5g/L) to maximize dissolved amylose for silky sauces.
- **International Day 1**: Full bilingual support (Italian & English) with metric (g, °C) and imperial (oz, °F) units, plus a detailed ingredient substitution guide for overseas cooks.
